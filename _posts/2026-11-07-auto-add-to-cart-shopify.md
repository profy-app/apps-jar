Learn why and when to use Auto Add to Cart in Shopify, and how to **automatically add free gifts** or discounted reward products to customers' carts when **they qualify for a Buy X Get Y offer**.

### Problems with Offering Reward/Free gifts in Native Shopify

Shopify’s built-in Buy X Get Y discounts **don't automatically add reward products to the cart** when customers add qualifying products. They also have the following limitations:

1. Customers must **manually add the free gift or reward product** to their cart after meeting the Buy X condition, creating **extra steps and potentially causing confusion**.
2. There is **no built-in way to promote or notify customers** about the free gift or reward product included in a Buy X Get Y offer.
3. Implementing Auto Add to Cart through custom code **requires additional programming**, making it complicated and **difficult to maintain**.

### Enabling Auto add Reward product to cart feature

In the **Widget Options** tab, enable the **Auto Add to Cart** feature. Once enabled, the reward product or free gift will be automatically added to the cart when the customer meets the Buy X condition, instead of displaying an upsell popup.

Once the offer is saved and activated on your Shopify store, the widgets promoting the Buy X Get Y discount will be displayed on your store.

The **Buy X Get Y offer widget appears on eligible product pages**, displaying the promotion details and instructions to customers.

When a customer adds a product to the cart and meets the Buy X condition, the **free gift is automatically added to the cart**, along with a notification confirming that the offer has been successfully applied.

Customers can also remove the automatically added free gift from their cart if they **don’t wish to claim it**, giving them the flexibility to proceed with their **purchase without the reward product**.
