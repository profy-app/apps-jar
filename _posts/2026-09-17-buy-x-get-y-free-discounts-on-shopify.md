Offering a “ **Buy X Get Y Free**” (BOGO) promotion is one of the most effective strategies to increase average order value and clear inventory on your Shopify store. But launching a successful campaign takes more than just turning on a discount — you need the right setup, targeted promotion, and clear performance tracking to drive real results.

In this guide, you will learn:

- **Creating BOGO Discounts:** How to set up Buy X Get Y Free promotions directly in Shopify Admin, or use a dedicated Shopify app for a simpler, more user-friendly, and effortless setup.
- **Promoting Your Offer:** How to use app widgets — like product page announcement banners, upsell popup, today’s offer widget and success notification — to make sure shoppers never miss the deal.
- **Tracking & Optimization:** How to analyze campaign performance through app analytics and fine-tune your offer strategy to maximize revenue and conversion rates.

Whether you’re looking for a simple native solution or an automated, high-converting app setup, this walkthrough covers everything you need to build, launch, and optimize your BOGO strategy.

### How to Create Buy X get Y Discounts Natively in Shopify (without App)

To create a native **Buy X Get Y** promotion directly inside your Shopify dashboard, follow these steps:

1. Log in to your Shopify admin panel and select **Discounts** from the left-hand navigation menu.
2. Click the **Create discount** button in the upper-right corner.
3. In the modal popup that appears, select **Buy X Get Y** as your discount type to open the detailed setup page.

<figure>
  <img src="/assets/blog/buy-x-get-y/discount-setup-page.png" data-zoom-src="https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:1400/format:webp/1*6XneYtkwyT-DQwBHw2t0_A.png" alt="Discount setup page" loading="lazy"
    decoding="async">
  <figcaption>Discount setup page</figcaption>
</figure>

Choose between a **Discount Code** (entered manually by the customer) or an **Automatic Discount**. Assign a unique title to identify the promotion.

1. **Customer Buys:** Set the trigger condition by specifying the required item quantity or minimum spend. Choose whether the rule applies to specific **Products** or entire **Collections**, set the purchase type (one-time, subscription, or both), and select the items using the **Browse** picker.
2. **Customer Gets:** Define the reward quantity and select the target products/collections. Set the reward value to **Free**, **Percentage Off**, or a fixed **Amount Off Per Item**, and specify the maximum reward uses permitted per order.
3. **Eligibility:** Restrict access to **All Customers** (default), specific **Market Segments**, **Customer Segments**, or individual **Specific Customers**.
4. **Maximum Discount Uses:** Set global limit rules, such as limiting the total number of times the discount can be redeemed overall or restricting it to **One Use Per Customer**.
5. **Combinations:** Choose whether this deal can be combined with other product discounts, order discounts, or shipping offers.
6. **Active Dates:** Schedule the exact start date/time and set an optional end date to automatically start and stop the promotion.

The main advantage of using the Profy Buy X Get Y + Free gift app over Shopify’s native setup is that **promotional widget controls are directly integrated** into the offer setup screen — whereas native Shopify only allows you to create the discount logic, leaving promotion entirely up to you.

The **Widget Options** tab allows you to enable up to four integrated widgets to promote your “Buy X Get Y” and “Free Gift” deals: **Today’s Offer Widget**, **Upsell Popup**, **Product Page BXGY Announcement**, and **Success Notification**. You can toggle any combination of these widgets to dynamically display and highlight your offer across your store.

**Advanced Configuration**

The **Advanced Configuration** section lets you schedule your offer to run continuously or set specific start and end dates. You can also restrict usage limits per order and configure discount combinations — allowing your offer to stack with shipping, product, or order discounts, just like in Shopify’s native setup.

To review performance:

2. Set your desired **Date Range** filter.

Once selected, the dashboard displays core metrics including **Total Sales**, **Average Order Value**, and **Total Orders**, alongside a visual sales and order performance graph plotted over time. Below the chart, a **detailed order log** lists every transaction attributed to the chosen offer, complete with direct links to view each order inside your Shopify Admin.

By tracking these analytics, you can easily **evaluate campaign health, compare performance** across different promotional strategies, and continuously test and optimize your offer rules to maximize store revenue.

### Conclusion

Creating a Buy X Get Y free offer in Shopify is straightforward, but running a successful campaign requires more than just enabling a discount rule. One of the biggest challenges for any Shopify store is effectively **informing customers about the offer at the right stage** of their shopping journey.

Because **native Shopify settings lack built-in tools to promote offers** directly on-site, choosing the right Shopify app is essential. By deploying integrated upsell widgets, you can engage shoppers from the very beginning of their purchase journey — **maximizing offer visibility and capturing more potential buyers** before they reach the checkout.

Finally, relying on clear analytics allows you to **measure each campaign’s real-world impact**. By tracking performance metrics and comparing offer variations, you can continuously refine your strategy, **optimizing what works and replacing what doesn’t** to drive consistent revenue growth.
